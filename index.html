All blocks

Tap a block to put it in slot 1. Number keys pick a different slot.

The islands

Tap anywhere on land to travel there (in Survival: villages, lighthouses, the castle and the temple). The white arrow is you. Gold ! marks someone with a quest for you.

Quests

Finish each island's quest, then bow to that island's god at the temple. When all six have blessed you, the way to the Hollow King opens.
